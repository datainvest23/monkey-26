Method, not magic

Why every ticker stays equally likely.

01 / REDEAL

Shuffle all 31

A fresh cryptographic Fisher–Yates shuffle creates a new order before every throw. The first 25 entries become the hidden wall.

02 / TARGET

Choose one of 25

The target cell is generated with rejection sampling. Values in the uneven modulo tail are discarded, so no cell receives a microscopic edge.

03 / VERIFY

Test the tickers

The dashboard computes a 31-category chi-square test and a normalised Shannon entropy score, rather than stopping at the less relevant cell distribution.

Interpretation. A p-value at or above 0.05 means the observed ticker mix is statistically consistent with equal 1/31 probabilities; it does not “prove” randomness. Small samples naturally look lumpy. This simulator is an educational experiment, not investment advice.
